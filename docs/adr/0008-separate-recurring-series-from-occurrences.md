# Separate recurring series from task occurrences

A recurring series and each of its occurrences have independent identities and version histories. Editing a series may regenerate untouched future occurrences, while any occurrence that has been individually edited, moved, completed, or otherwise changed becomes detached and remains unaffected by later series changes. Deleting a series removes untouched future occurrences but preserves past occurrences; detached future occurrences are reviewed individually, default to becoming standalone tasks, and may be selected for deletion with a strikethrough preview. Only concurrent changes to the same series or the same occurrence enter conflict resolution. This prevents schedule maintenance from overwriting work already performed or deliberately customized on individual dates.
