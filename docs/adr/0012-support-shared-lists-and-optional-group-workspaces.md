# Support shared lists and optional group workspaces

Groups may own shared lists as a standard collaboration capability and may optionally enable a complete group planner workspace. Personal planners remain private. Groups have exactly one Owner, zero or more Admins, and Members; no read-only role is initially supported. The Owner must transfer ownership to another current member before leaving, while a sole-member Owner may instead delete the group after fresh authentication and destructive confirmation; administrators cannot remove the Owner. A group owner or administrator may issue a single-use invitation link that expires after seven days by default and can be revoked before acceptance. The issuer may optionally bind it to a recipient email, requiring verified matching email at acceptance; otherwise its recipient accepts after sign-in or first-account creation. Acceptance always grants Member; only an Owner can promote the member to Admin. A shared task is unassigned or assigned to exactly one member, preserving one unambiguous shared completion state. The assigned member can place a linked reference in their personal plan: shared content, assignment, and completion retain one identity, while the member's personal day, time slot, order, reminder, and private note remain private. Removing a personal link does not delete the shared task. Voluntary departure may explicitly convert selected links to independent personal tasks before access ends; administrative removal instead unassigns group tasks and removes inaccessible links without granting new personal copies.
