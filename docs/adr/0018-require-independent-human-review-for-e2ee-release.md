# Require independent human review before production E2EE claims

End-to-end synchronization and groups may be developed and used in a self-hosted or personal beta after disciplined threat modeling, protocol-conformance tests, dependency monitoring, and explicit experimental-security documentation. Automated tools and AI agents may prepare those checks but do not substitute for independent human accountability or adversarial assessment. A qualified applied-cryptography and client-security professional or firm is required before presenting the feature as production-grade private collaboration or opening it to untrusted/public multi-user use; that review covers MLS integration, recovery and enrollment flows, local key storage, metadata exposure, authorization/revocation behavior, and native/browser boundary code.
